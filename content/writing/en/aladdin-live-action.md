Disney's 2019 live-action version of its classic animated *Aladdin* breathes new life into a story we all know. The visuals are stunning and the music is lovely, and it also carries a lot of deeper themes worth talking through with your kids, especially in the way it portrays women's autonomy.

With its colorful visuals and moving music, the live-action *Aladdin* vividly recreates Agrabah, a world full of fantasy. From the bustling market to the lavish palace, every scene is full of surprises and beauty. Will Smith's Genie brings plenty of humor, so adults and kids both get to laugh out loud [1].

The film lets parents and kids revisit a classic story together, and its excellent production lets the whole family feel the magic of movies. Kids will be swept up in Aladdin's adventure, while parents can find more layers in it, so everyone in the family gets something out of it.

In this live-action version, Princess Jasmine's character gets a lot more room to grow. She's no longer a princess waiting to be rescued, but a woman with ideals and ambitions [1]. Jasmine wants to become sultan and take on the responsibility of governing the kingdom, rather than simply marrying a prince [2].

In the film, Jasmine's new song, "Speechless," speaks directly to her fight for women's rights. Its central declaration, that she will not be silenced, is her voice pushing back against a patriarchal society [1].

Jasmine shows a strong sense of her own will and determination throughout the film. She no longer just waits to be saved. She bravely pursues her ideals, takes an active part in running the kingdom and works for the good of her people. You can see this clearly in several scenes.

**Taking part in discussions about the kingdom**: The live-action Jasmine doesn't just long for freedom. She actively joins discussions about how the kingdom is run, showing her political ambition and her ability to govern. This challenges the traditional definition of a woman's role and highlights her ambition and intelligence.

**Saying she wants to be sultan**: Unlike the animated version, the live-action Jasmine says plainly what she wants: to inherit the throne and govern the kingdom. That wish shows her independence and her determination to break through gender boundaries.

**Choosing her own marriage**: In the live-action version, Jasmine's marriage is no longer arranged by her father. She decides it herself. This emphasizes a woman's right to make her own choices about marriage and about her life.

Jasmine's strength and courage stand out in several scenes. The most memorable ones are these:

**Singing "Speechless"**: This song is one of the main ways Jasmine expresses herself. Its declaration that she won't be silenced is a strong protest against social injustice, and it traces her journey from helplessness to brave resistance.

**Standing up to Jafar's coup**: When Jafar seizes the throne, Jasmine refuses to bow to power and bravely fights for what's right. This scene highlights her sense of justice and her firm convictions.

**Finally becoming sultan**: At the end of the film, Jasmine takes the throne and abolishes the law that limited women's rights. This symbolizes a victory for women's autonomy and shows her determination to make her ideals real.

The live-action *Aladdin* has both the visuals and the music, and it's also a good chance for a family to talk about important social issues together. Watching it with our kids, we can help the next generation think about gender equality and spark their own pursuit of freedom and equality. The film shows that even a classic fairy tale can become a way of reflecting how far we've come.

- **Expectations and becoming yourself**

- Why do you think Jasmine wants to become sultan? In your own life, have you ever felt held back from a dream because of your gender?

- **Courage and perseverance**

- What kinds of courage does Jasmine show? Have you ever bravely said what you really think?

- **Equality and fairness**

- Why does Jasmine believe women should have the chance to rule? In our society, do men and women have equal opportunities?

- **Autonomy and choice**

- How does Jasmine show her autonomy? In your life, which decisions do you feel you should make for yourself?

- **Tradition and change**

- What traditions does the kingdom of Agrabah have? How do they affect Jasmine? Should every tradition be kept?

- **Leadership and responsibility**

- What qualities of a leader does Jasmine have? If you were a leader, what would you do to make things better around you?

- **Music and expressing feelings**

- What feelings does the song "Speechless" express? Is there a part of the song you like best?

- **Media and gender stereotypes**

- How is the live-action Jasmine different from the animated one? What does that change say about how society's view of women's roles has shifted?

These questions get kids thinking about important issues, and they also give parents and kids a good chance to really talk with each other. Moms and dads can pick the questions that suit their kids' age and understanding, and make the movie night that much deeper and more meaningful!
