I met Chih-Wei while doing my alternative military service. I was the most junior rookie for an incredibly long time, so long that I was almost done with my service before anyone came in below me. When Chih-Wei joined us, he was still handling company business as head of the MIX troupe. All I remember is that whenever I was working in the office, he was always revising proposals, sending off grant applications and sorting out the troupe's affairs, and he never slacked off on his official duties either. Just as he'd come in as an acrobat, he could keep a whole bunch of balls in the air and smile at you at the same time, a smile that warmed you up inside, because he was always sharing his dreams, whether about his own craft or about anything else he could think of!

Then I finished my service, and after that I could only follow Chih-Wei's journey through his Facebook updates. Then came the story we all know: FOCA was born, it changed Shezi Island, it went to Avignon, it had full-time acrobats, and it kept growing into more and more artistic territory, completely living up to the name Formosa. Then the pandemic hit. Even when things were at their hardest, I could see his gritted-teeth posts and all the choices he had to make. And now, as the pandemic lifts, heaven has handed out the most beautiful reward: a group of people completing this dream together.

I only know this small corner of FOCA, and I've only seen this one side of FOCASA, but a dream is made of more than one piece. Like a diamond, every facet has its own sparkle. In 2023 the stage lights hit this diamond, and the light it threw off left those of us watching dazzled and completely smitten.

For the sake of that sparkle, I used the one free day I had left and poured all the money I had into a single day of shows. I remember that the week before we went, the whole family watched *The Greatest Showman* three times, and I still very carefully told the kids: there won't be any elephants this time!

![Night at the FOCASA Circus Festival, a woman dancing with one arm outstretched in front of a red-and-white circus tent](/writing/vocus/focasa-circus-festival-2023/01.jpg)

Of course we went into the V-13 circus tent, where we saw [Tien Circus Theatre](https://www.facebook.com/Tien.Circus.Theatre)'s *Who Stole My Superpower?* (誰偷了我的超能力). We were in Section D, way off on the edge, but the story and the performance still had my eyes welling up. Maybe I've just reached the age where I get moved too easily. Still, I took a few sets of stronger photos while I was at it and sent them over, not sure whether they'd suit their taste.

![Three performers standing in the spotlight on a FOCASA Circus Festival stage](/writing/vocus/focasa-circus-festival-2023/02.jpg)

Then we moved on to the Popping Candy stage (跳跳糖). We watched every single Popping Candy show on the afternoon of February 26. The same techniques, but a different interpretation every time, and every one of them had a moment that got me: the tears of a dream coming true. I could see the performers on stage with tears shining in their eyes. I wonder if they could see us in the audience, moved to tears too?

I came back to Taipei with more than 1,400 shutter clicks: botched ones, ruined ones, ones where the settings suddenly went wrong, ones with dust on the sensor. A lot of the photos left me less than satisfied, and that made me ask myself: was I there to shoot, or to soak in the atmosphere? With a soul-searching question like that, the answer had obviously surfaced long ago. My wife kept saying she didn't want the holiday to end, and I didn't want to leave that dreamlike circus festival either.

So I made a quiet wish: next time, I hope I get the chance to be part of this big team. Hmm, maybe I should start by practicing jumping through flaming hoops...

![Two kids on the festival lawn, each lifting a leg in an acrobatic pose](/writing/vocus/focasa-circus-festival-2023/03.jpg)

His dream came true, or you could say the circus performers' dream came true, and it's incredibly exciting. Thank you so much to everyone who gave so much here. With everything they have, circus performers are teaching me what a dream is.

Thank you! Thank you!
